# Responsible use

This repository supports controlled academic measurement of Unicode/ASCII text
representation risks in chatbots. The transformation families are dual use.

Use the software only on models and services you own or are authorized to test. Review
provider terms, rate limits, billing, and data-retention policies before live runs.
Do not use the artefact to obtain harmful content from third-party systems, conceal
malicious instructions in operational workflows, or evade production safeguards.

The public result data intentionally omit prompt, response, adversarial-turn, trigger,
and rationale text. Do not attempt to reverse or augment the release into a catalog of
working harmful outputs. Researchers who need the private evidence for examination or
replication should request controlled access from the author and supervisor.

Report security concerns through the repository's
[private security-advisory channel](https://github.com/Lman7154/smuggle/security/advisories/new).
Do not include harmful payloads or secrets in a public issue.
